On top of that, the movie **resonated with me** deeply on a personal level. As I said, the doctor in the movie has **autism**, and when I was a kid, I also had a hard time expressing what I was thinking or feeling, which often led to **misunderstandings** and even conflicts with others. So watching it is like seeing my younger self, which makes me very emotional.

So yeah, _The Good Doctor_ is, **hands down**, one of the best movies I’ve ever watched. If I hadn’t seen it, I don’t think I would’ve realized just how meaningful and beautiful the profession of being a doctor can be.  
I’d **highly recommend** this movie to all my friends, especially those who are interested in medicine or who just want to watch something that’s heartfelt and **thought-provoking**.

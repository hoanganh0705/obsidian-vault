### **1.A time you used AI to complete a task:** 

Well, I’d like to tell you about a time I **missed** an **important plan** and something unexpected **came to my rescue**.

It happened last year when I was supposed to **prepare for** a **group presentation** in class. We had a **clear schedule**, and I was in charge of creating the **introduction**. But somehow, I completely forgot about it because I was so **caught up in** other assignments. On the morning of the presentation, my group asked me for the **introduction**, and I realized I hadn’t written a single word.

Needless to say, I **panicked**, but then I remembered I had access to a useful **tool**. I quickly typed in the **main topic** of our presentation and asked it to **generate** an **introduction**. Within seconds, it gave me a **clear and structured** opening paragraph. It was so helpful because it saved me hours of **thinking and planning**.

The best part was how **interactive** it was. I could ask **follow-up questions** like, “Can you give me examples for this point?” or “How do I explain this in **simpler words**?” The tool would respond instantly, and it made the whole process much **easier**.

I didn’t just **copy it directly**, of course—I made some quick edits and added a few **personal touches**. By the time the presentation started, I had something pretty good to share, and it saved me from completely **letting my group down**.

That experience taught me two things. First, I really need to **manage my time better** and not leave things until the last minute. Second, tools like this can be a **lifesaver** in tight situations, but you still need to use them **wisely**. It’s not about letting them do everything for you—it’s about using them as a tool to help you when you’re **stuck**.

1. **missed** (v) – bỏ lỡ
2. **important plan** (n) – kế hoạch quan trọng
3. **came to my rescue** (phrase) – đến cứu tôi
4. **prepare for** (v) – chuẩn bị cho
5. **group presentation** (n) – bài thuyết trình nhóm
6. **caught up in** (phr.v) – cuốn vào, bận rộn với
7. **panicked** (v) – hoảng loạn
8. **tool** (n) – công cụ
9. **generate** (v) – tạo ra
10. **clear and structured** (adj) – rõ ràng và có cấu trúc
11. **follow-up questions** (n) – câu hỏi tiếp theo
12. **simpler words** (n) – từ ngữ đơn giản hơn
13. **letting my group down** (phrase) – làm cả nhóm thất vọng
14. **manage my time better** (phrase) – quản lý thời gian tốt hơn
15. **stuck** (adj) – bị mắc kẹt

### **2. A journey you made on public transport that you enjoyed**

Well, I wanna tell you about a **remarkable journey** I took last year, and honestly, it turned out to be such an **unforgettable experience**.

I was **traveling to** a nearby city to **catch up with** a friend, and instead of **driving**, I decided to **hop on a bus**. At first, I wasn’t so sure about it—I mean, **public transport** can sometimes be **packed** or **stifling**. But that day, everything just felt right. The bus was **spotless**, the **air conditioning** was working perfectly, and I even got a **prime seat by the window**, which made me really happy.

As the bus started **cruising down the road**, I put on my **noise-canceling headphones** and played my **go-to playlist**. The combination of **soothing music** and the **breathtaking landscape** was amazing. The road was lined with **lush rice paddies** and quaint villages, and the **picturesque scenery** felt so peaceful. It reminded me how beautiful the **countryside** can be when you just take a moment to **take it all in**.

What made the trip even better was the **passengers**. There wasn’t a big **crowd** that day, so it felt **serene** and **laid-back**. I even had a short **conversation** with the person sitting next to me, which is rare for me because I don’t usually **strike up conversations with strangers**. They were **incredibly warm** and told me about some **hidden gems** in the city I was **exploring**, which I was **absolutely thrilled** about.

The **journey** wasn’t long—just about two hours—but it gave me this rare chance to **slow down** and appreciate the **little moments**. I remember thinking, “Why don’t I do this more often?”

Looking back, that **bus ride** wasn’t just a way to get from one place to another—it was an **eye-opening experience**. It taught me to **cherish** the **journey** just as much as the **destination**.

1. **remarkable journey** (n) – hành trình đáng nhớ
2. **unforgettable experience** (n) – trải nghiệm khó quên
3. **catch up with** (v) – gặp gỡ để trò chuyện
4. **hop on a bus** (v) – bắt xe buýt
5. **packed** (adj) – chật ních
6. **stifling** (adj) – ngột ngạt
7. **spotless** (adj) – sạch bóng
8. **prime seat by the window** (n) – chỗ ngồi đẹp bên cửa sổ
9. **cruising down the road** (v) – lướt đi trên đường
10. **breathtaking landscape** (n) – cảnh quan ngoạn mục
11. **take it all in** (v) – đắm chìm vào khung cảnh
12. **strike up conversations with strangers** (v) – bắt chuyện với người lạ
13. **hidden gem** (n) – những địa điểm ít người biết nhưng tuyệt vời
14. **cherish** (v) – trân trọng

### **3. A time you lost something important** 

I’d like to tell you about a time when I lost something really valuable—my house keys—after attending a **spectacular** music concert. It happened right after I’d finished a **nerve-racking** exam at school, which I managed to **ace**. To celebrate, my best friends invited me to join them for a concert featuring my **all-time favorite** singer. It sounded like the perfect way to unwind and **blow off some steam**, so I thought to myself, _why not?_ and immediately agreed.

When we got to the **venue**, I was **blown away** by how **jam-packed** it was. It was like being **squashed like sardines**—I could barely move. The moment the first song started, I completely **got lost in the moment**. I was jumping, waving my hands, and dancing like crazy along with everyone else. It was such an **electrifying** experience that I didn’t realize something had gone terribly wrong.

It wasn’t until the concert ended and I **reached into my pocket** to grab my house keys that I realized they were **nowhere to be found**. My **heart sank**. I checked my bag, **retraced my steps**, and even asked people around me, but no luck. I figured they must have **slipped out** while I was **getting carried away** with the music.

I had no choice but to call one of my best friends and ask if I could **crash at their place** for the night. Thankfully, they were super **understanding** and welcomed me with open arms. We ended up staying up late, **laughing our heads off** about the concert, watching the clips we’d recorded, and **reliving** all the **unforgettable** moments of the night.

Although losing my keys was a **major hassle**, it turned out to be a night I’ll never forget. The next morning, I got a new set of keys made, but I also learned my lesson—to **double-check my pockets** before heading out for another **wild night** like that!

1. **spectacular** (adj) – hoành tráng, ngoạn mục
2. **nerve-racking** (adj) – căng thẳng, áp lực
3. **ace** (v) – làm xuất sắc
4. **blow off some steam** (v) – xả hơi
5. **jam-packed** (adj) – chật kín
6. **squashed like sardines** (idiom) – bị ép chặt như cá mòi
7. **electrifying** (adj) – đầy phấn khích, sôi động
8. **get lost in the moment** (v) – chìm đắm trong khoảnh khắc
9. **nowhere to be found** (phrase) – mất tích, không tìm thấy
10. **slip out** (v) – rơi mất
11. **crash at someone’s place** (v) – ngủ nhờ nhà ai đó
12. **laugh one’s head off** (idiom) – cười sảng khoái

**relive** (v) – hồi tưởng lại

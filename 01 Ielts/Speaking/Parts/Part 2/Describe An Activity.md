### **1. A long walk you had** 

I’d like to tell you about a long walk I took not too long ago. A few months back, I was **knee-deep** in IELTS preparation, and let me tell you, it was anything but **a walk in the park**. I spent countless hours locked in my room, practicing speaking, writing essays, and drilling vocabulary. It was **mentally draining**, and at some point, I felt like I just couldn’t take it anymore. So, I decided to **hit pause** and go for a walk in the park near my house to **clear my head**.

The park is just **a stone’s throw away** from my place—barely a 10-minute walk. It’s **massive**, with a huge lake, towering trees, and plenty of open space for jogging, working out, or simply unwinding. It’s the perfect escape from the **hustle and bustle** of daily life.

When I got there, I started off with a **leisurely stroll** around the lake, just taking in the fresh air. After about ten minutes, I found a quiet bench and just sat there, listening to the birds **chirping away** and the leaves **rustling** in the breeze. It was incredibly **soothing**—for a brief moment, all my stress and worries about IELTS just **melted away**. No essays, no vocabulary lists—just pure peace.

As I was walking, I spotted a group of students studying together on a bench. They were **having a blast**, chatting and laughing while going through their notes and laptops. It made me realize that studying in the park might actually be a brilliant idea—you get to **soak up nature** while avoiding the usual distractions at home. Honestly, I wish I had thought of that sooner.

All in all, that walk was exactly what I needed. It **recharged my batteries** and left me feeling so much more refreshed and ready to tackle my studies again. It also taught me that sometimes, stepping away and taking a break is just as important as putting in the hard work.

1. **knee-deep in** (phrase) – chìm đắm trong
2. **a walk in the park** (idiom) – dễ dàng
3. **mentally draining** (adj) – gây kiệt sức tinh thần
4. **hit pause** (v) – tạm dừng, nghỉ ngơi
5. **clear my head** (v) – giải tỏa đầu óc
6. **a stone’s throw away** (phrase) – rất gần
7. **massive** (adj) – rất lớn
8. **hustle and bustle** (phrase) – nhịp sống hối hả
9. **leisurely stroll** (phrase) – đi dạo thư giãn
10. **chirp away** (phrase) – hót líu lo
11. **rustling** (v) – xào xạc (tiếng lá cây)
12. **melt away** (v) – tan biến (sự căng thẳng, lo lắng)
13. **soak up nature** (v) – tận hưởng thiên nhiên
14. **recharge my batteries** (idiom) – nạp lại năng lượng

### **2. Something you did that made you feel confident at your work or study**

I’d like to tell you about a tough decision I had to make a few months ago—whether to take on a part-time job while juggling my studies or to focus entirely on school.

You see, at that time, I was **at a crossroads**. On one hand, having a job would mean gaining valuable experience and a bit of financial independence, but on the other hand, it could take a toll on my studies. I was torn because I knew that if I took the job, I’d have to **stretch myself thin**, but if I didn’t, I’d miss out on a great opportunity. After a lot of **back and forth**, I decided to go for it, and let me tell you—it wasn’t exactly **a walk in the park**.

For starters, managing both work and school was a nightmare. I had assignments **piling up**, and after long shifts at work, I barely had any energy left to study. There were nights when I had to **burn the midnight oil**, trying to finish my schoolwork after getting home from my shift. Some days, I felt like I was **running on fumes** because I was only getting four or five hours of sleep. It was exhausting, both **physically and mentally**.

Another challenge was that I had to miss out on a lot of social activities. While my friends were out **having a blast**, I was either at work or buried in textbooks. There were moments when I seriously **questioned my decision** and felt like **throwing in the towel**. But every time I thought about quitting, I reminded myself why I started in the first place.

Looking back, I’m so glad I stuck with it. Not only did I **get the hang of** balancing multiple responsibilities, but I also became much more **disciplined** and **organized**. It taught me how to manage my time better, and honestly, I feel incredibly proud of myself for pushing through. It was tough, but it was definitely worth it.

1. **at a crossroads** (phrase) – đứng trước một ngã rẽ quan trọng
2. **stretch myself thin** (idiom) – tự làm mình kiệt sức vì quá nhiều việc
3. **back and forth** (phrase) – đắn đo, suy đi tính lại
4. **a walk in the park** (idiom) – dễ dàng
5. **piling up** (phrasal verb) – chất đống (công việc, bài tập)
6. **burn the midnight oil** (idiom) – thức khuya học bài/làm việc
7. **Run on fumes** (idiom) – hoạt động mà gần như hết năng lượng
8. **physically and mentally** (phrase) – về cả thể chất và tinh thần
9. **question my decision** (phrase) – hoài nghi quyết định của mình
10. **throw in the towel** (idiom) – bỏ cuộc
11. **get the hang of** (phrase) – quen với việc gì
12. **disciplined** (adj) – có kỷ luật
13. **organized** (adj) – có tổ chức, sắp xếp hợp lý

### **3. A water sport you would like to try in the future** 

I’ve always wanted to try surfing, and it all started when I came across this video on TikTok. It showed a girl surfing so **effortlessly** on these **massive** waves, and I was just **in awe**. She looked so **graceful** and **in control**, and I thought to myself, Wow, I really want to try that someday!

There are a couple of reasons why I think surfing would be so much fun. First, it’s the way it **connects with** nature. I mean, just imagine feeling the water under your feet, the wind blowing through your hair, and the freedom of riding a wave. It’s like you’re working with nature, not against it, and honestly, not many sports give you that feeling.

Second, I think it’d be a great way to **push myself out of my comfort zone**. Surfing is definitely **not a walk in the park** like badminton or football. It takes skill, balance, and a bit of **bravery**. To be honest, I’m a little **nervous** about it. I’ve seen movies where surfers get attacked by sharks, and that kind of freaks me out! But at the same time, I feel like the **thrill** and **sense of achievement** would totally be worth it.

If I ever get the chance, I’d love to learn in a place like Nha Trang or Da Nang, where the water is turquoise and the beaches are **gorgeous**. I know it won’t be easy at first, but with enough time and practice, I’m sure I’d **get the hang of** it. And who knows, it might even become one of my favorite hobbies in the future!

1. **effortlessly** _(adv)_ – một cách dễ dàng, không cần nỗ lực
2. **massive** _(adj)_ – rất lớn, khổng lồ
3. **in awe** _(phrase)_ – kinh ngạc, ngưỡng mộ
4. **graceful** _(adj)_ – duyên dáng, uyển chuyển
5. **in control** _(phrase)_ – kiểm soát tốt, làm chủ tình huống
6. **connect with** _(phr v)_ – kết nối với, hòa hợp với
7. **push myself out of my comfort zone** _(phrase)_ – tự đẩy mình ra khỏi vùng an toàn
8. **not a walk in the park** _(idiom)_ – không hề dễ dàng
9. **bravery** _(n)_ – sự dũng cảm
10. **nervous** _(adj)_ – lo lắng, bồn chồn
11. **thrill** _(n)_ – cảm giác phấn khích, hồi hộp
12. **sense of achievement** _(phrase)_ – cảm giác đạt được thành tựu
13. **gorgeous** _(adj)_ – đẹp rực rỡ, tuyệt đẹp
14. **get the hang of** _(idiom)_ – nắm bắt, quen dần với điều gì đó

## **4.Sample:** 

### **1. A place where everything is expensive:** 

Let me tell you about Circle K, a **convenience store** just a **stone’s throw away** from my house. It’s been around for ages, but I never really paid it much attention; I usually went to supermarkets instead. However, one day after school, I was **starving** and decided to grab a quick bite there. I picked up a sandwich and a drink, but when I got to the **checkout**, the total came to 50,000 VND. Honestly, I was quite surprised at how expensive it was for just a simple snack.

However, I soon understood why the prices were higher. Circle K offers a **wide range of** snacks and drinks, as well as ready-to-eat meals like **noodles** and rice, and the staff is more than happy to **heat them up** for you. This means you can enjoy a hot meal without the need to cook at home.

Plus, the staff are incredibly friendly and go out of their way to **cater to** your needs. I **vividly remember** looking for an **energy drink** but couldn’t recall its name, and a staff member **patiently** helped me find it, which I really **appreciated**.

The service is also exceptional. Being open 24/7 is perfect for late-night **cravings** or those working odd hours. Besides, the free Wi-Fi is a huge bonus, allowing you to enjoy your meal while **catching up on** work or studies.

So, yeah, despite the high prices, I think Circle K offers **good value for money**. The variety of products, the friendliness of the staff, and the exceptional service make it a place worth visiting.

1. **convenience store** (n) – cửa hàng tiện lợi
2. **stone’s throw away** (idiom) – rất gần
3. **starving** (adj) – đói lả
4. **checkout** (n) – quầy tính tiền
5. **wide range of** (phrase) – nhiều loại
6. **noodles** (n) – mì
7. **heat them up** (v) – hâm nóng
8. **cater to** (v) – đáp ứng
9. **vividly remember** (v) – nhớ rõ ràng
10. **energy drink** (n) – nước tăng lực
11. **patiently** (adv) – kiên nhẫn
12. **appreciated** (v) – đánh giá cao, biết ơn
13. **cravings** (n) – cơn thèm ăn
14. **catching up on** (v) – theo kịp, cập nhật
15. **good value for money** (phrase) – đáng đồng tiền bát gạo

### **2. A historical building:** 

Wow, this question is **putting me on the spot** because there are so many **historical landmarks** in Hanoi. However, if I had to pick one, I would like to tell you about the **Flag Tower of Hanoi**.

The first time I went there was after a **nerve-wracking** exam. My friends and I were looking for a place to **chill out**, and someone suggested grabbing a coffee at Highland Coffee, which is located right beneath the Flag Tower. It sounded like a great idea—good coffee and a chance to see one of Hanoi’s **iconic landmarks**.

So the Flag Tower has a very **basic structure**. It has a square base that looks like a **solid foundation**. The tower itself is tall and **slender**, with a **viewing platform** at the top where the Vietnamese flag flies proudly. The bright red flag with the yellow star really **stands out** against the sky. It’s simple but very **striking**.

As for what I want to learn, I’d love to know more about its meaning—like, what does it **represent**? Is it about strength, **resilience**, or something else? I also want to find out how it was built. How did they make it so strong that it could **stand the test of time**? What kind of materials and techniques did they use? And that’s about it—just understanding these things would be amazing.

In short, the Flag Tower is not just a **historical building**; it’s a **symbol of** Hanoi’s identity. Learning more about it would really help me connect with the city’s past.

1. **putting me on the spot** (idiom) – làm tôi bối rối
2. **historical landmark** (n) – địa danh lịch sử
3. **Flag Tower of Hanoi** (n) – Cột cờ Hà Nội
4. **nerve-wracking** (adj) – căng thẳng
5. **chill out** (v) – thư giãn
6. **iconic landmark** (n) – địa danh mang tính biểu tượng
7. **basic structure** (n) – cấu trúc đơn giản
8. **solid foundation** (n) – nền móng vững chắc
9. **slender** (adj) – mảnh mai, thon gọn
10. **viewing platform** (n) – bệ quan sát
11. **stands out** (v) – nổi bật
12. **striking** (adj) – ấn tượng
13. **represent** (v) – đại diện cho
14. **resilience** (n) – sự kiên cường
15. **stand the test of time** (phrase) – trường tồn với thời gian

### **3. A place you would like to learn the culture there**

First of all, I gotta say that I’ve always been fascinated by learning about the **culture** of other places or countries. But here I’d like to tell you about the **night market** in the **Old Quarter**, which I think is an **amazing** place to learn more about our **local culture**.

You know, my **interest** in learning about the **culture** there **stems from** a time when I was scrolling through TikTok one day, I came across a video that perfectly captured the **vibrant** atmosphere there – from the crowded stalls to the colorful lights and the lively energy that fills the air. Even though I’m a **local**, it made me feel like there’s still so much to discover, especially about the unique **cultural** aspects of the **night market**.

One thing that really **piques my curiosity** is the **bargaining** **culture** at the **night market**. **Bargaining** isn’t just about getting a better price; it’s a way for the buyer and seller to connect with each other. I feel like **bargaining** is like an “art” – if you’re witty and know how to make the seller laugh, you’re more likely to get a good deal. But if you’re too serious, it might not work in your favor/ you might not get what you want. If I could learn more about how people **bargain**, I think I’d understand more about the **local** lifestyle.

The second thing I’d like to learn more about is the **street food** **culture**, especially how it brings people together in such a casual, yet meaningful way. Specifically, I’m **interested** in the way **vendors** prepare and present the food, as well as the stories behind **traditional** **recipes**. For example, I once asked a **vendor** about her PHO recipe, and she told me it was **passed down** from her **grandmother**, with each generation adding a **personal twist**, which I find absolutely **fascinating**, and it makes me want to learn more about the origins of other dishes and how they’ve evolved over time.

I guess if I didn’t **explore** the **night market** one day, I would miss out on a chance to **reconnect** with my roots.

1. **culture** (n) – văn hóa
2. **night market** (n) – chợ đêm
3. **Old Quarter** (n) – khu phố cổ
4. **amazing** (adj) – tuyệt vời
5. **local culture** (n) – văn hóa địa phương
6. **stems from** (v) – bắt nguồn từ
7. **bargaining culture** (n) – văn hóa mặc cả
8. **vendors** (n) – người bán hàng
9. **traditional** (adj) – truyền thống
10. **PHO recipe** (n) – công thức nấu phở
11. **passed down** (v) – truyền lại
12. **personal twist** (n) – nét biến tấu cá nhân
13. **explore** (v) – khám phá
14. **reconnect** (v) – kết nối lại

### **1. Famous people:** 

- **Sport stars:** 

Well, **first of all,** I gotta say that I’ve always been a **die-hard sports fan,** especially when it comes to swimming. I absolutely love watching swimming competitions, and I really admire the athletes who **pour their hearts into** what they do. And if I had to pick one person who really stands out in this field, I'd like to tell you about **Nguyễn Thị Ánh Viên.**

Nguyễn Thị Ánh Viên is, **hands down,** one of the most amazing athletes I’ve known. I first learned about her when she was making headlines at the Southeast Asian Games, **breaking records left and right.** I gotta say that I was absolutely **blown away by** her talent. She truly **swims like a fish in its element.**

She’s not only an exceptional swimmer, but her dedication and perseverance are truly **second to none.** She’s incredibly driven and hardworking, practising **rain or shine,** and she never lets setbacks shake her confidence.

What I admire most about her is her humility. Even though her name is practically synonymous with success, she’s **not arrogant in the slightest.** She stays grounded and down-to-earth, making her a true role model for so many young athletes. Even after winning a race, she’s always there to support her competitors, often giving words of encouragement to those who didn’t make it to the podium. I really **hold her in high regard** for **being so compassionate and magnanimous,** always lifting others up even in her own moments of victory.

Whenever I watch her races, it’s like a **thrill ride!** I cherish those moments because they’re so energising. Every time she dives in, I’m **on the edge of my seat,** **cheering like I’m in the stands.** It’s amazing to watch her **push herself and go for gold.**

**All in all,** I’m **wholeheartedly grateful** to have an athlete like Nguyễn Thị Ánh Viên to look up to. Her commitment is so **inspirational and phenomenal** to me and so many others, and I can’t wait to see what she **pulls off** next.

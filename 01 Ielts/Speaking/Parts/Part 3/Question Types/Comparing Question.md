## **2. Language focus:** 

-  **_Sử dụng cấu trúc so sánh xuyên suốt câu trả lời_** 

1. **Ngữ pháp so sánh:**

|Cấu trúc|Ví dụ|
|---|---|
|So sánh ít hơn: Less + adjective/adverb/noun + than|Nowadays, people are generally less engaged with those living around them|
|So sánh nhiều hơn: More + adjective/adverb/noun + than (tính/trạng dài)|I think online learning is more flexible than traditional learning|
|So sánh nhiều hơn: Adjective/adverb + er + than (tính/trạng ngắn)|I'm not 100% sure but I guess children learn faster than adults|
|Tương phản với: As opposed to + Noun/Ving|Teenagers use smartphones most of the time as opposed to the elderly|
|So sánh với: Compared to / In comparison to + Noun/Ving|Compared to older generations, younger people are more tech-savvy|
|Trong khi A thế này thì B thế kia: S1+V1 Whereas/While S2+V2 (hoặc đảo lại)|Whereas Westerners may value straightforwardness, Asians may prioritize harmony and avoid confrontation|
|Ngược lại: In contrast/ On the contrary/ Conversely/ On the other hand, S+V|Wealthy countries enjoy stable economies. Conversely, poorer nations often face financial struggles|



2. **Các từ mang nghĩa so sánh:**

|Từ|Nghĩa|Ví dụ|
|---|---|---|
|Difference/ Distinction|Sự khác biệt chính (lưu ý: hai từ này có thể thay thế cho nhau)|Well, there's a clear distinction/difference between urban and rural lifestyles|
|Contrast|Sự tương phản|There is definitely a contrast in how the youth and the elderly spend their free time|
|Disparity|Sự chênh lệch|There is a significant disparity in income levels between the rich and the poor|

|Từ/Cụm từ|Nghĩa|Ví dụ|
|---|---|---|
|The former|Cái đầu tiên|"Who is happier, children or adults?" I lean towards the former since children are generally happier...|
|The latter|Cái thứ hai/ cái sau|...On the other hand, the latter, adults, tend to feel more stress due to work and family obligations|
|Lean towards/ Gravitate towards|Nghiêng về, thiên về (cái gì đó)|I lean towards the former since children are generally happier|
|Tend to V|Có xu hướng làm gì|Adults tend to feel more stress due to work and family obligations|
|Be inclined to V|Thích làm gì/ muốn làm gì (tích cực)|—|

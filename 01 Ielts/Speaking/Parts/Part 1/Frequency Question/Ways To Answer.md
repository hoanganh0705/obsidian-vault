### **Câu trả lời trực tiếp** 

- Hướng 1: Trả lời sử dụng trạng từ chỉ tần suất:


| Expression                                                                                                                                                                       | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Often/ usually/ regularly/ frequently do something**<br><br>Thường xuyên làm việc gì                                                                                           | **What do you do in your free time?**<br><br>I often paint.<br><br>I regularly paint.<br><br>I frequently paint.                                                                                              |
| **Whenever I can / as often as I can / whenever I get a chance**<br><br>Làm bất cứ khi nào có thể                                                                                | **When do you listen to music?**<br><br>I listen to music whenever I can.<br><br>I listen to music as often as I can.<br><br>I listen to music whenever I get a chance.                                       |
| **Without fail**<br><br>Chắc chắn làm, luôn luôn làm.<br><br>**Lưu ý:** Cụm này không đi 1 mình mà luôn đi với **every + N** để nhấn mạnh luôn luôn làm gì với tần suất như nào. | **When do you usually read?**<br><br>I wake up early and grab a book without fail every morning.                                                                                                              |
| **On a daily basis**<br><br>Ngày nào cũng làm                                                                                                                                    | **How often do you exercise?**<br><br>Well, I exercise on a daily basis.                                                                                                                                      |
| **Rarely do something**<br><br>Hiếm khi làm việc gì                                                                                                                              | **What do you usually do on the weekend?**<br><br>I often go out for a drink with my friends. I rarely stay at home.                                                                                          |
| **Every once in a while / every now and then**<br><br>Thỉnh thoảng                                                                                                               | **Where do you buy most of your clothes?**<br><br>Once in a while / every now and then, I head to the shopping mall near my house to browse for clothes.                                                      |
| **I don't V as much as I would like**<br>**I don't get round to V-ing as much as I would like**<br><br>Không làm gì được nhiều như mình mong đợi                                 | **Where do you usually read?**<br><br>I usually make good use of my commute to work to catch up on my reading on the bus. I don't read as much as I'd like; I don't get round to reading as much as I'd like. |
| **Once in a blue moon**<br><br>Rất hiếm khi                                                                                                                                      | **How often do you eat typical food from other countries?**<br><br>Honestly, once in a blue moon. I rarely get the chance, but when I do, it's always a special treat.                                        |
